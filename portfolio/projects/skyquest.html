01 / PRODUCT STORYTELLING

A clear story for a complex connection.

Editing, motion and product storytelling for SkyQuest × Galileo at Gogo.

PROJECT
SkyQuest
MY CONTRIBUTION
Editing, motion production + presentation
CONTEXT
In-house work / Gogo team

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make connection feel tangible.

Connectivity is a service people experience through the things it enables. The creative task was to connect aviation technology with a story an audience could see and feel.

02 / THE APPROACH

Find the story before the effects.

I organized testimonial footage into story categories and reusable selects. AI supported scripting and planning; I handled editing, building and presenting the work.

03 / THE DELIVERY

A story, carried through.
