02 / SPATIAL DESIGN

Think beyond the rectangle.

Flat-map graphics and assets for Gogo’s story on a very different kind of screen.

PROJECT
Sphere
MY CONTRIBUTION
Flat-map graphics + asset design
CONTEXT
In-house collaboration / Gogo

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Design for another dimension.

Gogo’s connectivity story needed to work on a curved, large-format surface. The assignment called for visual material that could move from a flat production layout into a venue experience.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Build the visual material.

I designed the graphics and flat-map assets, combining bold brand forms with imagery from a Gogo shoot. Those materials were prepared for the venue’s production team.

03 / THE COLLABORATION

Give every contribution its credit.

The Sphere team created the animation and final motion. My contribution was the graphics and flat-map assets. The installed campaign shown below is the result of that collaboration.
