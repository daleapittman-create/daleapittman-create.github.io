Design decision

One change at the menu can change the order.

A shift in service count affects portions, quantities and what the kitchen needs to prepare. SOUS explores those dependencies in a single kitchen-operations prototype.

The fictional Northstar trial moves from 48 to 96 covers. SOUS recalculates recipe quantities, compares demand with eligible inventory and prepares a simulated order. The scenario can be saved and restored as JSON.
