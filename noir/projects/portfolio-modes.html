Portfolio Modes

Design decision

Change the room around the image.

Natural, cyanotype and chiaroscuro became three ways to frame the same portfolio. The design question was how much atmosphere the surrounding page could carry while keeping the project legible.

I kept the project imagery in its original color and changed the presentation around it. A shared case-study structure carried the work across the different visual modes.
