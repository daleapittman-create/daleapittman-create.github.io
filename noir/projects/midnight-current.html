From the project

Midnight Current

Design decision

Let the movement set the atmosphere.

An ambient graphic can occupy the same spot for much longer than a title sequence. The pace and proportions of the movement become part of its character.

The local editor gives a maker controls for color, motion and canvas shape. A saved settings file carries the treatment forward, and a PNG records the composition.
