From the project

Corner / LED Studio

Design decision

Hold the camera still.

A corner display changes an image at the seam where two planes meet. A fixed viewpoint makes it easier to judge whether an image reads continuously across that break.

I built the study around one illustrated facade and a single 4:1 source. A designer can compare day and night, place media, and export a still from the same viewpoint.
