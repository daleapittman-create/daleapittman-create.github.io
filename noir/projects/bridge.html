BRIDGE / Luminous Field

Design decision

Keep the same map as the work changes.

BRIDGE brings projects, tasks and working context into one dashboard. The Luminous Field strip provides a stable place to return to when the active project changes.

A layer can expose a different part of the work while the strip keeps its position. This presentation follows one illustrative task through that change of focus.
