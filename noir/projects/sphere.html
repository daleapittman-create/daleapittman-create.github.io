Gogo at Sphere

Design decision

A rectangle was only the starting point.

On a desktop the graphic had edges. On the Sphere exterior it would be read across a curved surface at city scale. Large forms and the placement of the imagery had to carry the brand.

I combined imagery from a Gogo shoot with brand graphics and prepared the flat-map assets for handoff. The Sphere team animated the material and finished the venue motion.
