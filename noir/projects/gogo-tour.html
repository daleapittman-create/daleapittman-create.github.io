EU Demo Tour

Design decision

Where should the line break?

The European demo-tour wrap-up had to carry the Gogo mark and one inflight Wi-Fi message across three canvases. Reframing the same layout would have left the type fighting the edge.

In the portrait composition, “High Speed” sits above “INFLIGHT WI-FI.” The wider versions keep “High Speed” on one line. The After Effects capture shows all three decisions in the same workspace.
