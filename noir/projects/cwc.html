Design decision

Compose for the room.

An event audience encounters graphics while walking, turning, and looking across a room. At CWC, nonstandard LED formats shaped how Gogo’s identity could occupy the space.

I designed graphics and motion for those formats. The installed entrance gives the work a human scale and shows how the graphic system met the architecture.
