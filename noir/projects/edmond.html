Edmond

Design decision

What makes a wordmark feel like somewhere?

Edmond occupies a former Denver luggage warehouse. During my Ellen Bruss Design internship, I followed that history into baggage tags, ticket lettering and the marks that help a traveler find a destination.

The direction board put a wordmark, submark and directional arrows beside a proposed placard. My initial direction was selected, and the EBD team refined the final identity.
