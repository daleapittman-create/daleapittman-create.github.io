Glow Orb

Design decision

How much expression can one form hold?

A single orb makes small changes easy to see. Pulse, rotation and color become an interaction vocabulary without requiring a screen full of controls.

The archived browser prototype explores those behaviors through a WebGL path and a soft two-dimensional fallback. The image records the earlier fallback treatment.
