Motion Planning Toolkit

Design decision

Put the timing on paper first.

A motion project often becomes clearer when someone can sketch the sequence, mark the timing and name the assets in a single conversation.

Seven worksheets in an eight-page PDF give that conversation a form. They cover storyboards, timing, briefing, asset tracking, motion behavior and feedback.
