Design decision

The cut began in the interviews.

Aviation connectivity is hard to see on screen. For Gogo’s SkyQuest film, the people describing the service gave the edit its point of view.

I sorted testimonial footage by story, pulled reusable selects, and built the film around those voices. AI assisted with scripting and planning. I edited, designed motion, and presented the work.
