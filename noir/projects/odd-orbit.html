Design decision

Where does one episode end and the next begin?

Each Odd Orbit astronomy short starts with a question about an observed phenomenon. A diagram gives the narration something to point to, and the timed captions give the cut a reading rhythm.

The Moon and Uranus episodes are published examples of the format. The new filmstrip study follows the editorial handoff from an observation to a diagram and a timed cut.
