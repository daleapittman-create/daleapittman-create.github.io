Construct / RGB

Design decision

Separate the shape from the light.

A neutral object on a floor gives the eye a constant. Adjusting three lights then makes direction, color mixing and reflections easier to judge.

The WebGL prototype turns that arrangement into a small product stage. The exported still shows the relationship between the lit bevels and the reflected color underneath.
