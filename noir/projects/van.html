Galileo Demo Van

Design decision

Read the whole vehicle in one glance.

Gogo’s European demo van brought Galileo out of the presentation room and onto the road. The wrap had to read first as one image, then as graphic details on a vehicle.

The blue field and oversized satellite graphic carry across the breaks between body panels. In the Paris installation photo, the satellite still reads clearly from across the street.
