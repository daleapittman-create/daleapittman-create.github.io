Social Studio

Design decision

The artwork came first.

My mixed-media sketchbook drawing became the source for a family of social layouts. A portrait crop and a wide frame ask different things of the same drawing.

I built a browser editor for placement, type, and visual treatment. Layout rules carry the identity between formats, while the maker keeps control of the crop and hierarchy.
