Fieldguide

Design decision

Design for the visitor and the person at the counter.

A visitor wants a short list of places to go. A venue operator needs to keep the same local information useful on a sign. Fieldguide brings those two tasks into one pilot concept.

A visitor collects three stops into an itinerary. The operator can reuse the listings in a landscape sign. Workshop Market, a fictional venue, supplies the content for both sides of the demonstration.
